Principles

  • Ink on graphite

    The interface has no brand hue. Text is warm off-white on near-black; the only accent is the GT tricolour flag.

  • History is the trust

    Real photographs at natural brightness, with their printed credits. Facts carry their source on the page.

  • One face, two voices

    Archivo throughout: a condensed display voice (640, wdth 74) for titles and the normal text voice (wdth 100) for reading.

  • Phones get their own layout

    Below 700px the hero, the GT block and Le Mans are re-composed, not squeezed: the picture first, the words below it.

Colour

Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x against --ground. Everything set as text clears AA (4.5:1); --lm-blue is used for large type only.

Type

Archivo variable (wdth 62–125, wght 100–900) from Google Fonts; Cinzel only for the wordmark. Each role sets its own size, leading and tracking — set a role, never a size by hand. Display sizes are fluid between their clamp() limits.

Generations

    Pioneer dealer · Batavia, NY

  2. II

    Jim

    Mancuso Chevrolet · Skokie

    Opens Mancuso Chevrolet in 1953. A road rallyer, and a columnist for Ward's: “Mancuso on service”.

    Rick & Rob

    The dealership goes racing

    Sell cars with their father. Rick races from 1972 and in 1981 founds Lake Forest Sports Cars; Rob is 1986 SCCA Midwest Council champion.

    Adam

    Father and son on track

    Rick's son. 25 years at Lake Forest Sports Cars / Ferrari of Lake Forest as COO / VP. Go-karts at 12, Skip Barber Racing School at Road America; races Lolas alongside his father.

Rules

  • Do

    Use a type role from the scale; let each role carry its own leading and tracking.

  • Do

    Keep photographs at natural brightness, uncropped where the subject would be cut, with their credits.

  • Do

    Give every animation a still state that reads on its own (reduced motion, no script).

  • Do

    One primary button per block; the second action is a line button or a text link.

  • Don’t

    Set functional text below 14px, or long reading text in the display voice.

  • Don’t

    Use the tricolour anywhere but the label flag, or add a new hue to the interface.

  • Don’t

    Put words over a car on a phone: the picture goes first, the words below it.

  • Don’t

    Add a fact without a source.
