# Lake Forest Sports Cars — questions before publication

> **Update 2026-10-16 (from the manager):** Lake Forest Sports Cars has been SOLD. The GT is being created by a NEW company; its brand name is pending. The site must not present LFSC as the current maker. LFSC is part of the family history.

These are the open points from the first website prototype. Until each one is answered, the site either leaves the claim out or words it so it does not present an unconfirmed fact as confirmed.

## Facts in the 1976 chapter

1. **Sebring, 1976.** Fra Noi (Feb 2005) says that "also in 1976" Rick's Corvette was the first to break 200 mph on each lap of Sebring's back straight. Which event was that, and on what date? If it was the 12 Hours of Sebring in March, it came *before* Le Mans, and we will order the chapter that way.
2. **218 mph at Le Mans.** Fra Noi gives the speed on the Mulsanne Straight but not the session. Was it set in practice, qualifying or the race? The site currently says "June 1976" only.
3. **The #76 "customer car".** Our only source that the #76 was the first customer car John Greenwood built (for Rick), with a Chicago skyline on the hood, is the text on a Revell model-kit box. Can you confirm it, or point us to a primary source?
4. **The blue #76 photograph** (`RM 76`): where and when was it taken?
5. **Jim Mancuso's quote.** We quote Ward's Dealer Business (March 2001): "You're no longer just a car guy, you're a Mancuso, and I'm proud of you." Are you happy for it to appear, and may we name Rob as Rick's brother beside it?
