## 01 · The concept (`01-concept.png`)

Label: THE CONCEPT

**A vintage Corvette, transformed into a modern grand tourer.**

The GT concept is a dedicated build program for C2 and C3 Corvettes — engineered to be dependable, comfortable, and
genuinely daily-usable. Drive it to work, take it to dinner, or run it across the country without a second thought, all
while preserving the iconic shape that makes it a Corvette.

*Created and backed by Mancuso — over a century in sales, service, performance, international Corvette racing, and
collector vehicles.*

Card: THE PROGRAM SPANS TWO GENERATIONS
- Corvette C2 · 1963–1967
- Corvette C3 · 1968–1982

## 03 · The GT standard (`03-gt-standard.png`, ~00:47)

Label: THE GT STANDARD

**A comprehensive, structured refresh — every car, every generation.**
*Built around safety, drivability, comfort, and discreet modern technology.*

**Performance & Safety**
- Complete brake system renewal with modern friction materials
- Suspension & steering rebuilt; alignment tuned for radial tires
- Electronic ignition and a robust, high-output alternator
- Overbuilt cooling — radiator, fan & shroud, hoses, thermostat

**Comfort & convenience**
- Sound & heat insulation at firewall, floor, tunnel, bulkhead
- Modern-performance A/C and heat with strong, stable defrost
- Seats rebuilt with new foam and subtle long-distance contouring
- Period-correct appearance preserved throughout

**Everyday Use & Tech**
- Improved lighting; reliable windows, locks, wipers, washers
- Discreet Bluetooth audio, often behind a retro-style face
- Hands-free calling and hidden USB / USB-C charging
- Optional, subtly integrated backup camera where appropriate

## 04 · GT · C2 · 1963–1967 (`04-c2.png`, ~01:05)

***Timeless Shape, Effortless Drive***

C2s are inherently more collectible — so the focus is curated upgrades and tasteful reliability, while preserving
originality

- **Chassis & Brakes.** Frame and known stress points inspected and repaired. New four-wheel disc brakes with fresh lines;
  new bushings, joints, and gas shocks tuned ffor [sic] stability and comfort over harshness.
- **Powertrain & Driveline.** Mild, torque-focused calibration with electronic ignition. Meticulous carburetor setup — or
  discreet EFI on non-numbers-matching cars — plus overdrive to lower cruise RPM.
- **Cabin & Tech.** Targeted sound and heat control with original-style A/C controls retained. Re-foamed seats and a
  retro-look radio hiding Bluetooth, hands-free calling, and charging.

## 05 · GT · C3 · 1968–1982 (`05-c3.png`)

***The Long-Legged Corvette***

- **Chassis & Brakes.** Frame, birdcage, and body mounts addressed at known corrosion zones. Full suspension refresh tuned
  sport-touring — controlled yet compliant — with upgraded brakes and stainless lines.
- **Powertrain & Driveline.** Modern fuel injection as the standard spec for consistent running. Broad mid-range torque, a
  free-flowing low-drone exhaust, and overdrive matched to the rear gearing.
- **Comfort & Sound Control.** Extensive insulation at firewall, tunnel, and bulkhead to defeat heat-soak. Available
  high-output A/C, with interior upgrades including re-foamed seats and backup camera.

## What this means for the site

- **What the GT is:** a build program on customers' / sourced C2 (1963–67) and C3 (1968–82) Corvettes, not a new car.
  Name on screen: "Corvette GT — by Mancuso".
- **Fix on the homepage:** the GT section says "Late-’60s and early-’70s Corvettes". The film says C2 and C3, 1963–1982.
- **Imagery in the film:** pencil-style drawings of a C2 and a C3 and a blue "blueprint" of both. They are illustrations,
  not photographs of a GT build; they may be what the client called "too AI". Not used as product imagery.

### Covered by the film
- What the car is, the two generations, the promise, the standard refresh, the per-generation spec, who it is for,
  the heritage line, the name.
