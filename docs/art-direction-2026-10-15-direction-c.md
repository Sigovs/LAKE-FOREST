# LFSC · Direction C — "The team around the car"

Art-director's direction of 2026-10-15, **as amended by the user** the same day. Where the
two differ, the user's amendments (§ "Amendments") govern.

## Why the earlier work failed

`auction-editorial` — and the builder's reading of it — confined V1 and both sketches to one
archival aesthetic: a running head over every chapter (rail / sun strip / letterhead line), one
captioned print as the repeating unit, air as a status signal, native size as a virtue, period
lettering transcribed into type, ornament (rules, ghost numerals) instead of image, and the GT
deferred as a payoff. The vault agrees: no entry carries the dialect; its source,
`rmsothebys-com`, is rated 1 / out. **auction-editorial is out for LFSC.**

## Style mode

HYBRID — **anchor** `immersive-authored-world` (composition, rhythm, image behaviour, motion);
**contrast** `technical-luxury` (facts with consequence; at most one number per episode; no
spec grids); **signature** `expressive-poster`, one job only: the image-scale collision (small
native prints set against a full-width frame of the same world). Never used for type — no
bigger headlines. Unifying principle: evidence arranged as a crew around a car.

## Concept

LFSC launches its GT the way a race team launches a car — standing it among the people and
machines that earned it — so the archive reads as the team, not as the past.

## Revision 2 — message and structure (user, 2026-10-15)

**Page logic:** the Mancuso family / LFSC → a history of several generations → today's GT
project. **History is the main section.** The page is not a GT landing page and not a Corvette
archive.

- **Screen 1:** the name LFSC; one strong short line about the family in the automobile business
  since 1923; one sentence about the future GT. Primary action **Explore our history**, secondary
  **Discover the GT**. Unconfirmed geography and the phrase "building its own car" are excluded.
- **Screen 2:** the history itself begins — Benjamin, 1923. No family tree or link list before
  it. People are introduced as they appear in the story.
- **History:** the family and the business through several eras. Le Mans 1976 is one part of
  the road, at a smaller share than in sketch C. Chapters are not one repeated block.
- **GT:** a clearly marked future project — confirmed description and an enquiry. Archive cars
  never stand in for its visual; missing material is not covered by a large empty section.
- **After the history:** RPM Club / AM560 podcast, Instagram and the contact form, as in the
  brief. Their materials are missing (see "Missing material" below).

## Composition principles (user, 2026-10-15)

Applied inside this direction — no Japanese styling, no extra empty space.

- **Fukinsei — balanced asymmetry.** Photographs and text at different scales; deliberate
  offsets against the grid, held by a counterweight.
- **Datsuzoku — no repeated modules.** Every episode of the history is composed from its own
  content.
- **Shizen — naturalness.** Graphic decisions grow out of LFSC's real material: the cars, the
  liveries, the people and the family history — never imported ornament.
