The concept

A vintage Corvette, transformed into a modern grand tourer.

The GT concept is a dedicated build program for C2 and C3 Corvettes, engineered to be dependable, comfortable and genuinely daily-usable. Drive it to work, take it to dinner, or run it across the country without a second thought, all while preserving the iconic shape that makes it a Corvette.

Created and backed by Mancuso: over a century in sales, service, performance, international Corvette racing and collector vehicles.

The GT standard

A comprehensive, structured refresh: every car, every generation.

Built around safety, drivability, comfort and discreet modern technology.

Performance & safety

  • Complete brake system renewal with modern friction materials
  • Suspension and steering rebuilt; alignment tuned for radial tires
  • Electronic ignition and a robust, high-output alternator
  • Overbuilt cooling: radiator, fan and shroud, hoses, thermostat

Comfort & convenience

  • Sound and heat insulation at the firewall, floor, tunnel and bulkhead
  • Modern-performance A/C and heat with strong, stable defrost
  • Seats rebuilt with new foam and subtle long-distance contouring
  • Period-correct appearance preserved throughout

Everyday use & tech

  • Improved lighting; reliable windows, locks, wipers and washers
  • Discreet Bluetooth audio, often behind a retro-style face
  • Hands-free calling and hidden USB / USB-C charging
  • Optional, subtly integrated backup camera where appropriate

C2

GT · C2 · 1963–1967

Timeless shape, effortless drive.

C2s are inherently more collectible, so the focus is curated upgrades and tasteful reliability, while preserving originality.

Chassis & brakes

Frame and known stress points inspected and repaired. New four-wheel disc brakes with fresh lines; new bushings, joints and gas shocks tuned for stability and comfort over harshness.

Powertrain & driveline

Mild, torque-focused calibration with electronic ignition. Meticulous carburetor setup, or discreet EFI on non-numbers-matching cars, plus overdrive to lower cruise RPM.

Cabin & tech

Targeted sound and heat control with original-style A/C controls retained. Re-foamed seats and a retro-look radio hiding Bluetooth, hands-free calling and charging.

1969 Corvette C3 Stingray. Photo: Sicnag, CC BY 2.0

C3

GT · C3 · 1968–1982

The long‑legged Corvette.

Chassis & brakes

Frame, birdcage and body mounts addressed at known corrosion zones. Full suspension refresh tuned sport-touring, controlled yet compliant, with upgraded brakes and stainless lines.

Powertrain & driveline

Modern fuel injection as the standard spec for consistent running. Broad mid-range torque, a free-flowing low-drone exhaust, and overdrive matched to the rear gearing.

Comfort & sound control

Extensive insulation at the firewall, tunnel and bulkhead to defeat heat-soak. Available high-output A/C, with interior upgrades including re-foamed seats and a backup camera.

Who the GT is for

For drivers who love the soul of a vintage Corvette, but demand the manners of a modern one.

  • Drive it, don’t store it.

    A Corvette for the commute, not just the occasional sunny weekend.

  • Take the real trip.

    Long road trips, in real weather, with real confidence.

  • Give up nothing.

    Classic styling with reliable starts, working instrumentation, strong brakes and quiet highway manners.
