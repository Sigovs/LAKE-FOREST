## 1. Design Read

- **Deliverable / audience / family** — homepage sketch, Hero → History → GT announcement,
  for collectors and LFSC clients; **contemporary motorsport launch**.
- **Mandate** — REDESIGN. Carried untouched: the names, the crest only as photographed,
  real photographs at natural brightness and never upscaled, sourced facts only, the agreed
  story (family → its own GT), Lenis.
- **Dialect** — HYBRID: anchor `immersive-authored-world` (composition, rhythm, image
  behaviour, motion) / contrast `technical-luxury` (facts with their consequence, at most one
  number per episode) / signature `expressive-poster` (image-scale collision only — never type).
  `auction-editorial` is OUT: no compatibility with a launch; it confined V1 and both sketches.
  Unifying principle: evidence arranged as a crew around a car.
- **Dimensionality role** — ABSENT for 3D; depth from photographs and scale. Motion is
  scroll-linked support (motion-designer owns it).

## 2. The concept

LFSC launches its GT the way a race team launches a car — standing it among the people and
machines that earned it — so the archive reads as the team, not as the past.

**Concept Gate: PASS** — CG1 opens on a business act; CG2 one sentence, falsifiable; CG3 swap
the composed episodes for a timeline and it reads as "the past"; CG4 the static build holds;
CG5 drops running heads, dated plates, serif/mono, rules, period type; CG6 one direction;
CG7 every episode and the GT carry it. Historical cars stay in the history; the GT is
announced with confirmed facts only.

## 4. The peak (`DNA28`)

"The family that raced Corvettes at Le Mans is building its own GT." The page culminates in
the GT announcement; inside History, the blue #76 is the chapter's own climax.

## 5. Page grammar (`DNA36`)

**Chaptered scenes** — each episode composed from its own material (split stage, full-bleed
frame + people, evidence row), not one module repeated. Differs from V1 (dated plates on a
rail) and the sketches (running strip / letterhead entries): no running head, no repeated unit,
photo rows used once, not as the page's pattern.

## 6. The signature move (`DNA37`)

The image-scale collision: small native prints set against a full-width frame of the same
world, so the prints read as inserts beside a master shot. Motion is designed by
motion-designer against this composition (see §7, MOTION READ in its report).
